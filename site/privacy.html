Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy covers the Koalino apps for Android, Linux, Windows and macOS, the Koalino server, and the Koalino browser extension. Koalino is free software made by volunteers; its source code is public, so anyone can check what it does.

In short

The people who make Koalino receive none of your data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reports or tracking. Your meetings stay on your devices, unless you choose to send them somewhere, as described below.

What Koalino stores on your devices

  • Meetings: transcripts, notes, summaries, photos, files and projects, in a database encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is kept in your system's keychain (Android Keystore, Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain, or GNOME Keyring or KWallet on Linux).
  • Audio: recorded speech is deleted once it has been transcribed. If you turn on Keep meeting recordings (off by default), it is kept encrypted on that device until you delete it.
  • Voices: when you name a speaker, Koalino keeps a voice profile, a list of numbers describing the voice, not the audio. Profiles are encrypted and can be deleted at any time.
  • Measurements of how fast transcription and summaries run on each of your devices, used to choose which device does the work. They stay among your own devices.

What Koalino sends, and only when

  • To your other devices: devices you pair with a QR code sync directly with each other over an end-to-end encrypted connection, on your local network or through a relay you run on one of your own devices. No Koalino server is involved.
  • To Hugging Face: when you choose to download a transcription model, the app downloads it from whisper.cpp's page on huggingface.co. Hugging Face sees your IP address, as with any download.
  • To Qualcomm: on Android phones with a supported Snapdragon chip, if you choose to run transcription on the NPU, the app downloads Qualcomm's files from Maven Central and Qualcomm AI Hub.
  • To an AI provider you choose: summaries and chat are off until you set them up. If you connect a provider (for example OpenAI, Google, Mistral or a model on your own computer), the text of the meetings you summarize or ask about is sent to that provider and handled under its privacy policy. If you use your own Claude Code or Codex installation, Koalino runs it on your computer and it talks to its provider under your account.
  • Backups: Koalino's backups are encrypted with a passphrase you choose before they leave the app, and go to the folder you pick, which can be a cloud storage folder. Android's own cloud backup is turned off for Koalino.
  • Exports and shares: files you export or share go where you send them.

The browser extension

The extension records Microsoft Teams meetings you join in the browser, after you agree in each meeting. It reads the call's audio, the live captions and transcript Teams shows you, and slides shared on screen (never cameras), and passes them only to the Koalino app on the same computer, through the browser's native messaging. Meetings the app cannot take yet wait, encrypted, in Koalino's own helper on that computer. The extension sends nothing to any other server; it stores only its settings and a list of the meetings it sent (titles and times, kept two weeks).

Permissions

  • Microphone: to record meetings, only while you record.
  • Notifications: to keep recording with the screen off and show its controls.
  • Camera: to take photos for a meeting, when you choose to.
  • Network: to sync with your devices and for the downloads and providers above.
  • Biometrics: to unlock the app, if you turn on the app lock.
  • Running in the background: to finish transcribing a meeting while charging, if you allow it, and to run scheduled backups.

Other people in your meetings

Recording a meeting records other people. Ask for their consent and follow the rules where you are. Their data is stored and sent only as described above.

Your control

Everything Koalino keeps is on your devices: you can delete meetings, voices and recordings in the app, and uninstalling it removes its data. As the makers of Koalino hold no data about you, there is nothing for them to give you or delete.

Children

Koalino is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published here and in the source repository's history. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
